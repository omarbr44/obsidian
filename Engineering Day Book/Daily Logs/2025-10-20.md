# 2025-10-20

## 🛠️ Work Problems
- **Issue**: Sidebar height exceeded viewport when combined with header, causing unwanted scrollbars.
- **Fix**: Replaced `height: 100vh` with a Grid layout that accounts for the header. Alternatively, `position: fixed` worked but was less flexible. 
- **Lessons**: `100vh` ignores other elements in flow → leads to overflow.
- `position: fixed` takes out the element out of the normal document flow (so will be in the viewport edges)
- `overflow: hidden` only hides _internal_ overflow, not page overflow.
- Flexbox/Grid is the cleanest way to make layouts that adapt to headers/footers.
